# After Forty — design brief

## Base

Use the shared Design System at [`../_base/Nocturne/`](../_base/Nocturne/). This folder is an adaptation layer only and must not copy or modify base files

## Documented direction

The existing identity material identifies After Forty as a blog hosted by **Heidi Braun**, for an audience 40+, in English, for the US/global market, published by FBR News at `afterforty.fbr.news`

The existing editorial categories are:

- Skin & Beauty
- Recovery & Wellness
- Home Fitness

The existing identity material also describes Heidi as writing in first person, recommending only what she has used for more than a month, and placing a source at the end of every health claim. The blog transparency note says it is supported by advertising and affiliate commissions, without pre-publication brand approval

## Adaptation boundary

The source material records a light mode, Newsreader, an 8px radius and a hue value of 42 for this preset. These are documented inputs, not permission to invent a new palette, logo, imagery system or component set. The override file therefore changes only the type/radius values that map directly to existing tokens; all other values remain inherited from Nocturne until separately approved

Health, legal and publishing requirements remain content and governance concerns. The official disclaimer is mandatory as recorded in the handoff material
