## 2. Os 4 Pilares Fundamentais do Projeto

1. **Nicho:** Local Retail & Brick-and-Mortar Small Business Marketing
2. **Subnicho:** Storefront Signage, Point-of-Sale (POS) Displays & Foot-Traffic Conversion (*Store Signs & Displays*)
3. **Problema Central:** Small business owners waste their physical touchpoints as static space instead of active media, losing daily foot traffic, suffering from low average order value (AOV) and failing to capture customer contacts at the counter.
4. **Audiência Alvo nos EUA:**
   - *Street Retail:* Coffee shops, bakeries, boutique shops, pet stores.
   - *Reception & Services:* Hair salons, wellness clinics, local gyms, accounting/real estate offices.
   - *Food & Dining:* Diners, food trucks, casual eateries.
   - *Trade Shows & Events:* Craft fair vendors, pop-up markets, expo exhibitors.
