# 7 Storefront Mistakes That Make Customers Walk Past

A storefront must answer quickly: What is this? Is it for me? Is it open? What can I do next?

## The seven mistakes

1. The logo is visible, but the offer is not.
2. Too many posters compete for attention.
3. Text can be read only from very close.
4. Opening hours are missing or outdated.
5. The call to action is vague.
6. The message does not match the audience or moment.
7. Nothing is measured after the change.

## A 15-minute correction plan

Stand where a customer first sees the business. Write down what you understand in three seconds. Remove one competing message, rewrite the main message in one sentence, add one clear next action and record a baseline before changing the sign.

An A-frame, window graphic, open sign or counter display can help after the communication problem is clear. None guarantees sales.
