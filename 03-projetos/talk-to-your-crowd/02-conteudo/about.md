# About Talk to Your Crowd

Talk to Your Crowd is an educational publication from FBR Agency about turning everyday customer touchpoints into useful sales and lead-generation media.

We start with the business problem, explain the strategy, identify the physical moment and show how to measure the next test. Store Signs & Displays are practical implementation tools, not the subject of every lesson.

The publication serves businesses and entrepreneurs in the United States and Canada. Content is published in EN-US.
