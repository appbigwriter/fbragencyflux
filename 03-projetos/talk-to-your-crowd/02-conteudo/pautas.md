# Editorial Line and Initial Content Calendar
## Talk to Your Crowd

**Market:** United States and Canada  
**Language:** EN-US

## Editorial thesis

Every business has a physical customer touchpoint that can work as media: storefront, counter, reception desk, table, booth, window or sidewalk. Start with the business problem and sales strategy. Show the sign, display or printed piece only as the practical implementation.

## Content pillars

1. **Universal sales fundamentals:** attention, clear offers, real proof, reciprocity and customer journey.
2. **Attract:** storefront visibility, A-frames, windows, hours and the first three seconds.
3. **Convert:** counter prompts, menu hierarchy, add-ons, price communication and placement.
4. **Capture:** QR codes, reviews, loyalty and permission-based follow-up.
5. **Segment playbooks:** retail, food, salons, professional services, fairs and pop-ups.
6. **Measure:** baselines, UTMs, offer codes, scans, sign-ups and weekly tests.

## Article acceptance checklist

- [ ] The reader understands the business benefit without knowing a product category.
- [ ] Examples work for at least two business contexts.
- [ ] Claims are cited, qualified or framed as a testable hypothesis.
- [ ] No invented numbers, testimonials or rankings.
- [ ] One clear CTA and one measurement action are present.
- [ ] Product mentions include alternatives and disclosure when applicable.
- [ ] SEO title, slug, headings and meta description match intent.

## Editorial rule

Store Signs & Displays are tools. The editorial subject is the customer decision and the strategy that improves it.
