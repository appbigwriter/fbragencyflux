# About Game Style

Game Style is an editorial guide to gaming gear, setups and accessories. We explain what a product is for, which constraints it solves and where its trade-offs matter.

Recommendations are contextual, dated and transparent. We separate official specifications, testing, editorial judgment and user reports. A higher price or larger specification is not automatically a better fit.

When we use affiliate links, we disclose that relationship clearly. Editorial independence includes recommending an alternative—or telling you to keep your money—when a purchase does not solve a real problem.
