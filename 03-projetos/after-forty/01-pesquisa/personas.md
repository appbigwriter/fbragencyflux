# After Forty — Audience Personas v0.1

**Priority lens:** Women 40+ without excluding other adults 40+.  
**Editorial standard:** evidence-led, practical, non-alarmist, and free of guaranteed weight-loss or appearance claims.

## The Evidence-Seeking Rebuilder

Adults 40–58 rebuilding routines around skincare, strength, sleep, mobility, and realistic progress. They need plain-English evidence, limitations, safe next steps, and transparent disclosures. They distrust miracle language, shame, and unsupported supplement claims.

## The Midlife Skin Minimalist

Adults 45–65 seeking a smaller routine for dryness, sensitivity, texture, and sun exposure. Content should prioritize gentle cleansing, moisturizer, sun protection, ingredient compatibility, and consistency over product volume.

## The Sustainable Home Athlete

Adults 40–65 who prefer walking, low-impact training, bands, or dumbbells. Content needs beginner progressions, modifications, recovery guidance, and stop/seek-care signals without guaranteed fat-loss promises.

## The Midlife Health Navigator

Women and other adults 40–65 seeking respectful information about sleep, stress, energy, and changing health needs. Content must not diagnose, assume menopause status, or promote hormone hacks; it should help readers prepare questions for a qualified clinician.

## Rules for every article

- Never assume gender, menopause status, skin tone, income, disability status, fitness level, or medical history.
- Do not promise rapid weight loss, reversal of aging, guaranteed results, or disease treatment.
- Separate general education from diagnosis and treatment.
- Cite authoritative or primary evidence and state limitations.
- Include professional-care boundaries where symptoms, medication, pregnancy, or medical conditions may change the recommendation.
- Place affiliate disclosure near commercial recommendations.
