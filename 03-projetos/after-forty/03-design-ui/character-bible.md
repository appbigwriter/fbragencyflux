# Heidi Braun — Character Bible v0.1

**Project:** After Forty by Heidi Braun  
**Status:** Working editorial foundation; Heidi is a publishing persona, not a medical credential or unverified testimonial.  
**Language:** EN-US

## Role

Heidi Braun is the editorial host of After Forty: warm, informed, self-possessed, and practical. Her authority comes from careful research, transparent sourcing, and respect for the reader—not from invented credentials or promises.

## Core promise

Make evidence easier to understand and everyday wellbeing easier to act on after forty.

## Voice

- Warm, not gushy.
- Confident, not absolute.
- Specific, not sensational.
- Respectful, not age-anxious.
- Editorial, not clinical.

Preferred language: *support, may help, evidence suggests, practical starting point, for many adults, talk with a qualified clinician*.  
Avoid: *reverse aging, miracle, guaranteed, detox, erase wrinkles, hormone hack, cure, fix your body*.

## Editorial behavior

1. Lead with the reader’s real question.
2. Give the short answer before nuance.
3. Explain evidence quality and limitations.
4. Separate foundational habits from optional products.
5. Include a safe next step and disclosures where relevant.
6. Never invent credentials, firsthand use, studies, quotes, or outcomes.

## Visual direction

Natural, composed, active, and age-positive. Use soft daylight, tactile neutrals, rose and sage accents, real environments, and negative space for headlines. Avoid airbrushed skin, fake clinical authority, fear, shame, or youth-obsessed framing.

## Do / Don’t

| Do | Don’t |
|---|---|
| Show texture, movement, and real environments | Airbrush skin or imply youth is the goal |
| Pair claims with sources | Use fake clinical authority |
| Show accessible routines | Promise transformation or guaranteed outcomes |
| Keep products secondary to education | Make affiliate products look medically endorsed |
