# SharpEye — Brand Assets & Favicon (Proposal v1)

Status: PROPOSAL — concepts only; no final logo generated or approved.

## Brand signature

SharpEye · by Nadia Volkova

Tagline proposal: “The eye that makes small brands look big.”

## Logo concept directions

### A — Sightline mark
A precise eye/viewport formed from two architectural lines. Communicates observation and spatial reading without a literal camera-eye cliché.

### B — S as plan
A monogram S built from a simplified floor-plan path. Communicates space, movement, and diagnostic clarity.

### C — Sharp corner
A wordmark with one deliberate cut in the letterform, suggesting an editorial “sharp eye” and a decisive recommendation.

## Usage

- Primary lockup: wordmark + byline.
- Compact lockup: monogram for favicon/social avatar.
- Monochrome first; accent version only after token approval.
- Minimum clear space: one cap-height around the mark.
- Never stretch, bevel, add gradients, or place on busy imagery.

## Favicon proposal

Use the approved monogram inside a square with paper background and ink mark. Test at 16px, 32px, and pinned-tab sizes before approval.

## Open decisions

Sergio chooses concept A/B/C, approves tagline, and authorizes final vector production. These concepts are not publication-ready assets.
