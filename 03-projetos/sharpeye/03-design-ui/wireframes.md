# SharpEye — Home & Article Wireframes (Proposal v1)

Status: PROPOSAL — structure for discussion; no implementation implied.

## Global navigation

Logo | The Teardown | Guides | Comparisons | About Nadia | Search

Persistent utility: source/date labels, disclosure, and a visible “Send a space for review” pathway. Do not activate collection until privacy/consent rules are approved.

## Home

1. Header: signature, navigation, disclosure link.
2. Hero: consequence-led promise — “Make the right people stop.” One-sentence explanation and CTA to latest Teardown.
3. Featured Teardown: large image/diagram, observation, one priority fix, evidence label.
4. Three editorial lanes: Stop / Read / Move.
5. Latest guides: cards with intent label, reading time, evidence status.
6. Decision block: portable vs modular vs custom vs rental.
7. Product relevance: only contextual, clearly labeled, and sourced from the approved catalog.
8. About/disclosure block: fictional/AI persona disclosure and editorial limits.
9. Footer: privacy, terms, disclaimer, affiliate disclosure, contact.

## Article template

- Breadcrumb and intent label.
- Title framed around consequence, not object.
- Standfirst: who it helps and what decision it supports.
- Evidence strip: source mode, date, limitations.
- Main body with scannable H2s.
- Diagnostic callout: observation / hypothesis / recommendation.
- Visual example or diagram with alt text.
- “What to test next” checklist.
- Related article and relevant product only if justified.
- Disclosure and sources.

## The Teardown template

1. Permission/status label.
2. Space context and objective.
3. Five-second read.
4. What attracts / disappears / confuses.
5. Single highest-leverage change.
6. What is hypothesis versus observed fact.
7. No-shame closing and test plan.

## Responsive behavior

- Mobile-first single-column reading.
- Desktop editorial grid with 680–760px text measure.
- Preserve evidence labels and disclosures above the fold where relevant.
- Avoid carousels for core evidence; all content must remain keyboard and screen-reader accessible.

Approve navigation labels, homepage CTA, submission/consent flow, and whether product modules appear in v1.
