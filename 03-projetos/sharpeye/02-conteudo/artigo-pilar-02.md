# SharpEye — Pillar Article 02 (Proposal)

Status: DRAFT — requires editorial review and quantitative SEO validation.

Working title: Portable, Modular, Custom, or Rental? How to Choose a Trade Show Display
Intent: comparison/commercial investigation.

Decision framework:
- Event frequency and reuse.
- Footprint and venue restrictions.
- Message-change frequency.
- Storage, shipping, drayage, and installation.
- Need for portability versus bespoke spatial impact.
- Budget as a constraint, not a promise of outcome.

Outline:
1. Start with the event pattern, not the product.
2. Portable: speed and lower complexity.
3. Modular: reconfiguration and reuse.
4. Custom: distinctive spatial expression.
5. Rental: flexibility and reduced ownership burden.
6. Comparison matrix.
7. Questions to ask before requesting a quote.

Evidence plan: use ExpoMarketing’s public rental/modular framing and Lunne’s experiential-design positioning as vendor examples, clearly labeled; do not generalize vendor claims into universal savings.

Publication Gate: catalog validation, pricing/fulfillment review, source review, disclosure, and human approval.
