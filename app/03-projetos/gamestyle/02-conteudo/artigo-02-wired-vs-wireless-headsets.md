# Wired vs Wireless Gaming Headsets

**Status:** Draft for product and compatibility verification; EN-US/global.

Wired headsets simplify charging and can offer predictable compatibility. Wireless headsets add freedom of movement but also battery, pairing, firmware and receiver variables. Neither is universally best.

## Compare the decision points

- Charging: wired avoids battery management; wireless requires a charging routine.
- Connection: verify the exact connector, receiver, Bluetooth mode and platform support.
- Movement: wireless reduces cable management; wired may be simpler at a fixed desk.
- Ownership: consider replacement cables, battery aging, receiver replacement and warranty.
- Fit: check glasses comfort, microphone behavior, controls and replacement parts.

Choose wired for simplicity and multi-platform standard connectors. Choose wireless when cable freedom is valuable and you accept battery management. Keep the current headset if the actual issue is routing, fit or a missing adapter.
