# TT-005 — Video Transcript (EN-US)

**Video:** *I Built a Capsule Wardrobe for My Actual Life (Not My Imaginary Calendar)*  
**Source:** `02-conteudo/tt-005-capsule-wardrobe.md`  
**Status:** local production artifact; not a publication authorization.

## 0:00–0:20 — Hook

“Welcome to the closet of a woman in her thirties: six black tops, three versions of the same hopeful outfit, and one dress waiting for an event that has not happened since 2019. Today we are building a capsule wardrobe for real life—not a beige personality transplant.”

## 0:20–1:30 — Audit

“These are not boring clothes. These are evidence.”

“Start with the places your clothes need to take you during a normal week. Look at the pieces you actually reach for, and make a short repeat list before you buy anything.”

## 1:30–3:00 — The roles

“Create five sections: everyday, work, movement, social, and weather or travel. The number of pieces depends on your lifestyle and climate. There is no universal formula.”

## 3:00–4:15 — Three-question filter

“Before buying, ask three questions. Can it make three outfits with what I already own? Does it serve a real calendar situation? And would I choose it on a tired Tuesday?”

“If the answer is no to two of these, leave it in the shop. Your bank account is allowed to have boundaries too.”

## 4:15–5:00 — Accessories

“Show how one accessory changes two existing outfits. Choose by use first: comfort, durability, storage, weather, and how often the item will leave the house. Avoid must-have language.”

## 5:00–5:40 — What I’d do

“I would wait a week before buying anything, photograph the outfits I actually wear, and purchase only the missing role. The goal is fewer decisions, not a closet that wins an argument online.”

## 5:40–6:00 — Disclosure and close

“This video may include affiliate links. If you shop through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost. The links are in the description, and none of them are a commandment. If an item does not earn its place, skip it and save your money.”
