## Description plan

1. **Opening closet shot (0:00–0:20):** Describe the visible wardrobe at a useful level: clothing arranged in a closet, with the presenter introducing the real-life capsule concept. Do not describe body size, age as a defect, or appearance as a problem.
2. **Weekly-use audit (0:20–1:30):** Describe the presenter selecting frequently worn pieces and placing them together to show a repeat list.
3. **Role groups (1:30–3:00):** Describe clothing grouped by everyday, work, movement, social, and weather/travel roles. State that the groups are examples, not a universal required quantity.
4. **Three-question filter (3:00–4:15):** Describe each candidate item being considered beside existing clothes; the on-screen text should match the three questions in the transcript.
5. **Accessories (4:15–5:00):** Describe one accessory being shown with two existing outfits. Include functional details only when visible, such as storage or how the item is worn; do not claim durability or quality without verification.
6. **Closing wardrobe plan (5:00–5:40):** Describe outfit photographs or a written repeat list if shown. Avoid before/after framing and avoid implying that clothing changes a person’s worth.
7. **Disclosure (5:40–6:00):** Keep the spoken affiliate disclosure in the transcript and captions. Any on-screen disclosure must be readable and placed near the relevant recommendation if links are later approved.

## Production requirements

- Use body-neutral, non-sexualized framing.
- Do not use before/after, age-shaming, fear-based or shame-based visuals.
- If products are later added, describe only what is visibly shown and separately verify price, availability and product claims.
- Add human-reviewed captions before publication; this VTT is a local draft aligned to the current script.
- Keep product links, catalog claims and monetization outside this package until validation and the applicable Gate.
