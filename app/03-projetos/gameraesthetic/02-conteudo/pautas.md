# Editorial roadmap & content briefs — Gamer Aesthetic

**Market:** EN-US / global  
**Status:** initial editorial plan; product facts, prices and availability require verification at publication.

## Editorial promise
Gamer Aesthetic helps gamers make better-informed choices about gear, setups and accessories. Every recommendation must explain the use case, trade-offs, compatibility, limitations and when the reader should skip the purchase.

## Required article structure

1. Clear answer and intended reader.
2. Date of verification and market/platform scope.
3. Selection criteria and method.
4. Trade-offs and limitations for every recommendation.
5. Compatibility, dimensions, connectivity and warranty checks.
6. Lower-cost, used/free or “skip this purchase” alternative.
7. Affiliate disclosure close to recommendations.
8. Separation of official specifications, testing, editorial opinion and user reports.
9. Last reviewed date and update trigger.

## Editorial guardrails

- Never promise better aim, rank, FPS, health or guaranteed performance.
- Do not publish prices, stock or compatibility without date-specific verification.
- Do not invent tests, benchmarks, reviews, testimonials or scientific claims.
- Ergonomics content must remain educational and non-diagnostic.
- “Best” requires explicit criteria and a defined audience.

## Production sequence

1. Validate keyword/SERP evidence for the selected brief.
2. Confirm platform, country and budget scope.
3. Gather dated primary product specifications.
4. Draft using the required structure.
5. Run compliance, affiliate and factual QA.
6. Obtain the applicable publication gate before external release.
