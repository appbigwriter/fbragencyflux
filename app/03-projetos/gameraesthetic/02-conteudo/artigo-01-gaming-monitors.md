# Best Gaming Monitors by Setup, Platform and Desk Size

**Status:** Draft for dated product verification; EN-US/global.

The right gaming monitor depends on platform, viewing distance, desk depth, GPU output and budget. Start with those constraints before comparing refresh-rate numbers.

## Selection criteria

- Native resolution and viewing distance.
- Refresh rate supported by the platform and GPU.
- Variable refresh support and exact compatibility.
- Panel behavior, contrast, response consistency and finish.
- Inputs, stand adjustment, warranty and total cost.

Higher refresh rate does not guarantee better aim or performance. Higher resolution can increase GPU load, and HDR labels vary in practical quality. Verify current model specifications, price, availability and warranty before publication.

## When to skip the upgrade
Keep the current monitor if it already matches the platform output and the proposed upgrade adds no meaningful solution to a stated problem.
